Using the app

From a clip to an MP4.

  1. 01

    Add your video

    Click the video area or drag a clip into the app.

  2. 02

    Choose a file size

    Keep 19.5 MB for Discord’s 20 MB limit, or set your own size.

  3. 03

    Compress your clip

    Choose where to save it. Check the result, then use Show in folder to find it.

The app keeps the full clip and checks its size before saving. Your original file is kept. The result shows its file size and compression time.
